# Northwind Health

Synthetic payer for Northwind Imaging. There is no real plan id, no portal, and no clearinghouse route.

Timely filing in this demo is **90 days from the date of service**. The filing deadline shown on a denial is that date, or it is blank when the remit did not carry one. The readiness gate treats a blank deadline as missing.

The demo clock is 17 November 2026. Deadline risk is overdue, urgent within 14 days, soon within 45 days, otherwise comfortable.
