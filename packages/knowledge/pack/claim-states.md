# Claim states

`draft`, `scrubbed`, `submitted`, `accepted`, `paid`, `denied`, `appeal-ready`, `appeal-submitted`, `upheld`, `overturned`, `closed`.

A PR-1 deductible line is patient responsibility. It does not enter `denied` for appeal. An OA-23 line is informational and is not worked as a denial. `appeal-ready` is allowed for a CO-50 line only after the readiness gate passes.
